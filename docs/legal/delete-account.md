# Delete your Tick ToDo account

Tick ToDo (package `com.ferrabytes.ticktodo`, developer FerraByte) lets you
delete your account and all of its data from inside the app:

1. Open Tick ToDo and go to **Settings** (the account icon at the top right).
2. Scroll to **Account** and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm, and sign in with Google once more when asked (this proves it is
   you).

What is deleted, immediately:

* every task, list and tag stored in your account on our servers (Cloud
  Firestore);
* your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication (Google account id, e-mail,
  name, profile picture URL);
* everything the app stored on that phone.

What is kept: nothing tied to your account. Aggregated, anonymous analytics
events already recorded (app opens, screen views) cannot be attributed to you
and are not deleted.

If you no longer have the app installed, e-mail **ferrabyte512@gmail.com**
from the Google account you used to sign in and ask for deletion; we will
confirm within 30 days.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).
