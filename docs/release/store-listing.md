## Full description (max 4000)

```
Tick ToDo is a simple, fast to-do list that helps you plan your day, remember what matters and actually get it done.

TASK SCHEDULING
• Add a task in seconds: the keyboard opens straight away, and Today, Tomorrow, a date, priority and list are one tap away
• Type #tags right in the title
• Due dates and times, priorities, lists, tags, notes and subtasks
• Repeating tasks: daily, weekdays, weekly, every two weeks, monthly or yearly
• Today, Upcoming, Inbox and Completed views, plus search, sorting and bulk actions
• Undo for completing and deleting

REMINDERS THAT ARRIVE ON TIME
• Exact reminders at the due time or minutes, hours or days before
• Snooze or complete straight from the notification
• A built-in Reminder health checklist shows exactly what Android settings could delay your reminders, and takes you to the fix
• Reminders come back automatically after your phone restarts
• Task names stay hidden on a locked screen

FOCUS TIMER SESSIONS
• Turn on a focus timer for any task you choose: it is never forced on every task
• Work in focus rounds with short breaks and a longer break after a few rounds
• Pick a ready-made plan (25/5, 50/10, 15/3, 90/15) or set your own lengths and number of rounds
• Pause, skip or stop, and mark the task done when you finish
• The timer keeps running when you leave the app, with a live countdown in the notification
• See how long you focused today and on each task

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Today & overdue
• Upcoming (next 7 days)
• Task list: show any list, tag or view you choose
• Quick add: one tap to capture a task
• Focus timer: see and control your running session

WORKS OFFLINE, SYNCS EVERYWHERE
• Everything works without a connection; changes sync when you are back online
• Sign in with Google to keep your tasks on all your Android devices
• Your tasks are private to your account

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Large, easy-to-tap task cards
• Six colour themes, light and dark mode, and optional wallpaper colours
• Works with TalkBack, large text and tablets

No ads. Delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings.

Questions or feedback: [support email]
Privacy policy: [privacy policy URL]
```
