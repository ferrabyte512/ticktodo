# Store listing text (en-GB)

Paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.

## App name (30)

```
Tick ToDo
```

## Short description (80)

```
A calm to-do list with alarm reminders, a focus timer and a priority matrix.
```

## Full description (4000)

```
Tick ToDo is a to-do list that keeps your day clear.

Write a task, give it a date, and it appears under Today when it matters. Tick it off and move on. Nothing is hidden behind menus and nothing gets in your way.

WHAT YOU GET

Today and Upcoming
See what is due now and what is coming next, with a progress ring that fills as you tick tasks off.

Reminders that really ring
A reminder rings like an alarm at the exact minute you chose, with sound, vibration and a notification. Snooze it or mark it done without opening the app.

Priority matrix
Sort tasks by urgent and important in a four-box view, so you always know what to do first.

Repeat, subtasks, lists and tags
Daily, weekday, weekly, monthly or yearly repeats. Break a task into steps. Group tasks into lists and tag them any way you like.

Focus timer
Start a focus session on any task, take breaks, and add minutes when you are in the flow.

Works offline, syncs everywhere
Add and edit tasks without a connection. Sign in with Google and your tasks follow you to every Android device.

Home-screen widgets
Today, Upcoming, any list, quick add and the focus timer, right on your home screen.

Your colours
Pick one accent colour and the whole app follows it, in light and dark mode.

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

PRIVACY
Your tasks are stored in your own Google-linked account and are never sold or shared. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time. Full policy: https://ferrabyte512.github.io/ticktodo/legal/privacy-policy.html
```

## Screenshot order

Phone, upload all eight in this order: `01` Today (dark), `02` Matrix (dark), `03` Focus (dark), `04` Reminder notification (light), `05` Today (light), `06` Task editor (light), `07` Upcoming (light), `08` Appearance (dark).

Look used for the captures: accent **Sage**, background **Plain**, no wallpaper, no keyboard. Dark cards sit on a deep green gradient, light cards on a pale mint one; headline and one-line subline above a rounded phone screen that bleeds off the bottom. Rebuild with `compose.py` from the raw captures in `raw/` if a screen changes.

Tablet slots (7-inch and 10-inch): Play accepts these same 1080×1920 cards; both sides are within the allowed range. Use them unless real tablet captures are made.
